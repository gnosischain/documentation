# Full Investing Suite

Every component switched on: spending, savings, card, tokenised assets, transfers and secured credit against the same account.

**Who it is for.** For brokers and neobanks adding tokenised assets and self-custodial crypto.

**Status.** Design partners

## What it switches on

| Component | Replaces |
| --- | --- |
| Account | core banking and a sub-ledger vendor |
| Euro Money | a deposit account and safeguarding |
| Yield | a money market fund rail or partner-bank sweep |
| Card Settlement | float, processor and BIN sponsor |
| Assets | broker, custodian and CSD |
| Fx & Transfers | a treasury desk and correspondents |
| Secured Credit | Lombard lending |
| Identity and Rewards | batch AML and a loyalty ledger |

## 0-1 guides

Work through these in order. Each ends in something you can see on the block explorer; if a step does not produce the stated result, stop there rather than continuing.

### 1. [Deploy your first contract](/start/guides/deploy-your-first-contract)

Get a verified contract on Gnosis. Every other route starts here.

### 2. [Accept stablecoin payments](/start/guides/accept-stablecoin-payments)

Take euro-denominated payment into a contract you control.

### 3. [Launch a tokenised asset](/start/guides/launch-a-tokenised-asset)

Issue a capped-supply token bound to an off-chain asset.

:::note
Secured credit and FX are configured with Gnosis engineers. Read the scope note in the tokenised asset guide before issuing anything to anyone.
:::

## Talk to the engineers

Bundles are configured with the Gnosis team rather than self-served end to end. The [ecosystem directory](https://build.gnosischain.com/) lists who is already live on each component.
