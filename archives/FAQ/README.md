# Node & Validator FAQs (Archived)

:::danger Archived
These pages are **no longer published**. `/faq/node` and everything under `/faq/Node FAQs/` now
redirect to [Gnosis Chain FAQs](https://docs.gnosischain.com/faq/others).

They are kept for historical reference only. Deposit and withdrawal mechanics, client versions,
monitoring endpoints and staking parameters in these pages have not been maintained.
:::

The FAQ section now publishes a single master FAQ,
[Gnosis Chain FAQs](../../docs/faq/others.md), with the bridge questions folded in as a Bridging
subsection. Node and validator setup guides are published under
[Run a node](https://docs.gnosischain.com/node) until the move to Gnosis EEZ. For current guidance
see [validategnosis.com](https://www.validategnosis.com/) or [Discord](https://discord.gg/gnosis).
